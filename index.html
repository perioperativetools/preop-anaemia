Pre-operative Anaemia Decision Support

Adult elective/scheduled surgery prototype: enter the Hb, trend and a small anaemia screen, then generate a nurse action, a focused senior-review question and copyable communications.

LOCAL GOVERNANCE PROTOTYPE · V2
Decision-support only. This tool does not “clear” a patient for surgery and does not create an automatic cancellation or transfusion threshold. The nurse initiates locally authorised pathway actions and escalates defined questions; treatment outside the nurse-led pathway and surgery timing remain clinician decisions. Refer to the current pre-op Hb-SOP.
