Local governance prototype · adult elective / scheduled surgery

Pre-operative Anaemia Decision Support

Three-column workflow: identify the trigger → interpret what it means → generate the right action and communication.

Decision-support only. This tool does not “clear” a patient for surgery and does not create an automatic cancellation or transfusion threshold. Nursing staff should initiate only locally authorised pathway actions and escalate defined questions. Surgery timing, treatment outside the nurse-led pathway, specialist referral and transfusion remain clinician decisions. Do not enter patient-identifiable information into a public demonstration version.

Clinical routing

Route the question, not a generic “clearance” request: perioperative/anaesthetic review for timing or blood-loss planning; GP/primary care for investigation/treatment continuity; haematology for complex cytopenia/film, haemoglobinopathy, haemolysis, difficult blood or unexplained/severe anaemia.

Evidence/governance basis carried forward from the existing prototype: CPOC perioperative anaemia guideline 2025; BSH adult preoperative anaemia guideline 2024; current local pre-op Hb-SOP and local transfusion/medicines governance. Hb <130 g/L is treated here as an optimisation trigger for major/high surgery, not a universal cancellation or transfusion threshold. Prototype for local validation before clinical deployment.
